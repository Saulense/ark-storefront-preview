GOOD QUESTIONS.
CLEAR ANSWERS.

What’s the difference between ARK Melts Original and ARK Melts Extra Strong?

ARK Melts Original is your everyday clean-focus melt — smooth, light, and easy to use for work, study, social energy, or a simple caffeine upgrade. ARK Melts Extra Strong is the stronger version — made for bigger days, harder sessions, heavier workloads, early starts, and the moments where a cute little boost is not going to cut it.

Can I take ARK Melts Extra Strong before a workout?

Yes. It’s perfect when you want that pre-workout style switch-on without the shaker, powder, sugar, or heavy stomach feeling. Take one before training when you want more energy, sharper focus, and a clean push into the session.

Can I take ARK Melts Extra Strong for work or studying?

Absolutely. Extra Strong is made for deep work, long calls, deadlines, studying, editing, planning, creating, and every moment where your brain needs to stop wandering and start performing.

How often can I take ARK Melts Extra Strong?

We recommend starting with 1 melt a day . Extra Strong is built for the days when you need a bigger kick, so start with one, feel the effect, and listen to your body. If you tolerate caffeine well, you can use it when you need that extra switch-on — just don’t stack it blindly with coffee or energy drinks.

When’s the best time to take ARK Melts Extra Strong?

Whenever regular energy isn’t enough. Take it before a heavy work block, a workout, a long study session, an early start, or any moment where you need to wake up fast, lock in, and actually get moving.

Does ARK Melts Extra Strong contain caffeine?

Yes — ARK Melts Extra Strong contains 100mg of natural caffeine . The difference is how it feels. It’s designed to deliver fast, clean energy without the ugly caffeine side effects people hate — no sugar crash, no shaky spike, no coffee breath, and no heavy energy drink feeling.

Who is ARK Melts Extra Strong for?

For people who need more than a soft little boost. If coffee makes you crash, energy drinks make you jittery, or regular caffeine feels messy, ARK Melts Extra Strong is built to give you fast energy, sharper focus, and a smoother ride — without the sugar-loaded nonsense.

Will ARK Melts Extra Strong replace my coffee or energy drink?

Yes — and for many people, that’s the whole point. ARK Melts Extra Strong gives you the energy and focus you want from coffee or energy drinks, but without the sugar, bloating, crash, jitters, or waiting around. No cup. No can. No drama. Just melt and go.

How fast does ARK Melts Extra Strong work?

ARK Melts dissolve directly on your tongue, so they’re made for fast absorption and quick switch-on. No brewing, no mixing, no sipping for 20 minutes — just one melt and you’re ready to move.

Does ARK Melts Extra Strong cause jitters or a crash?

No — ARK Melts Extra Strong is designed to give you clean energy and sharp focus without the jitters, anxiety spike, or crashy comedown that many people get from coffee and energy drinks. It’s a stronger hit, but smoother and cleaner.
